### Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. An original editorial blog cover, landscape 16:9, at least 1600x900, exactly 16:9.

Art direction: a detailed inked editorial illustration, clean confident line work with light cross-hatching, flat colours with soft painterly shading, in the spirit of a European graphic novel. Night-time interior, warm lamp light against deep navy and teal shadows, a few small amber and teal light accents. Hand-made feel, not photorealistic, not glossy 3D, not a neon tech network.

Subject: an engineer's wooden desk on the last night of the year. In the centre, a tall, slightly leaning stack of thick science and engineering books, one of them open on top with abstract diagrams of orbits, gears and waveforms. Beside the stack, a tablet propped on a stand showing a simple physics experiment: a pendulum swinging in front of a plain background. Around them: a small brass telescope, a tiny model rocket, a mechanical gear puzzle, a mug, a closed notebook with a pencil. A desk lamp lights the scene. Through the window behind, a quiet town at night with a few small distant fireworks in the sky, clearly secondary to the desk.

Composition: the book stack and the tablet are the focal point and read clearly at thumbnail size; the subject occupies the central 75% with breathing room and works under a centred crop to a wide banner as well as a small card. One strong idea readable at thumbnail size: on New Year's Eve, the desk is full of science books and a physics video while the fireworks stay in the background.

Constraints: no people, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no readable text anywhere (book spines, pages and screens carry only abstract marks). No recognisable characters or brands.
