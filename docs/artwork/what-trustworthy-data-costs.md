# What Trustworthy Data Costs — cover artwork

Five editorial covers for PR #39. Generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-01.

## Series direction

Painted architectural cutaways: matte gouache, plaster grain, restrained stencil texture, mineral strata and readable control points. Navy, petrol, sage, sandstone and coral retain the blog's system-mapping identity. Each cover focuses on a different constraint; no generated lettering or logos.

The covers are mapped by article slug in `PostEditorialArt.tsx` and shared by both locales. They do not replace the article's technical figures. The edited articles are prepared for scheduled release from 2026-10-26 to 2026-11-09 in both locales.

## Assets

`src/assets/images/posts/editorial/<slug>-source-960.{avif,webp}` and `<slug>-source-1600.{avif,webp,jpg}`.
